# Prompted Oy — legitimate interests assessment (marketing website only)

**Prepared:** 22 October 2026 · **Status: DRAFT, not signed off or adopted** · Controller: Prompted Oy (3550114-7), Turku, Finland.

This assessment addresses ONLY the new public `prompted.fi` marketing website and inbound business enquiries. It is **not** an assessment of any authenticated `app.prompted.fi` product, employee learning data, third-party purchased leads, cold outbound marketing, CRM enrichment or analytics campaigns.

## Legal test and decision rule

GDPR Article 6(1)(f) requires **all three** conditions: a legitimate specified interest; processing necessary for that interest (with less intrusive methods considered); and a balance that does not favour the individual's overriding rights or interests. EDPB Guidelines 1/2024 on legitimate interests provide detailed guidance, issued as a consultation draft. This worksheet is not legal sign-off and cannot turn a future proposed policy into actual current practice.

### Activity A: Replies to voluntarily initiated B2B enquiries

| Criterion | Preliminary assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Defined legitimate interest | Answer a natural person's voluntary service question; explore requested business cooperation; prepare a proposal requested by the sender. |
| Types of data | Sender name, work email, voluntary organisation/title, message and follow-up messages. No website form, browsing identity graph or automated profiling in current site source. |
| Necessity and less intrusive method | To respond asynchronously, a reply address and the relevant request are needed. A visitor can read the services and try the static Capability demonstration without providing personal data. Avoid collecting unnecessary data; company/title optional. |
| Reasonable expectations | Someone emailing a public B2B contact address can reasonably expect a reply about the question. This **does not** authorise automatically adding them to a newsletter, unrelated sales campaigns or a purchased prospect list. |
| Potential impacts | Disclosure of professional contact data, inbox/service-provider exposure, unrequested continued contact, retained message content and possible unexpected sensitive personal information. |
| Safeguards proposed for verification | Need-to-know inbox access, encrypted transport where supported, no routine profiling, respect opt-outs and GDPR objections, remove accidentally received sensitive material where appropriate, apply a genuine documented retention/review practice. |
| Balance | **Provisionally favourable only for proportionate responses to voluntary messages.** Final decision conditional on verifying access controls, retention and any provider transfers. Any materially broader use requires a separate lawful-basis review. |

Where a natural person asks Prompted Oy to take steps toward a contract *with that same individual*, GDPR Article 6(1)(b) may be relevant instead; confirm facts rather than assuming every B2B enquiry automatically qualifies.

### Activity B: Website delivery and security logs

| Criterion | Preliminary assessment |
| --- | --- |
| Defined legitimate interest | Deliver the public website securely, detect abuse and keep services available. |
| Possible data | IP address, URL and time, device/browser and security or error logs generated by Vercel hosting. No site-coded ad pixels, external analytics SDK, user account or submission form in the reviewed static source. |
| Necessity and alternatives | Technical request handling is ordinarily needed to deliver content. Collect only data that the actual host requires; avoid adding a separate tracking system. Check whether logs can be reduced or retained for a shorter period. |
| Reasonable expectations and potential impact | Short technical logging for service reliability may be expected. Extended identifiable traffic histories, third-party transfers or use for advertising create additional privacy risks and are **not** approved by this analysis. |
| Safeguards requiring factual confirmation | Actual Vercel project settings, edge and access-log types, retention, geographic processor/subprocessor access, DPA scope, cookie/device-storage inventory and incident access governance. |
| Balance | **Undecided** until real hosting configuration and processing locations are confirmed. Do not treat this row as completed legitimate-interest balancing. |

## Measures that are explicitly outside this assessment

Outbound unsolicited prospecting, email marketing lists, cross-site advertising, employee or customer learning evidence, AI model prompts, per-person productivity analytics, purchased third-party contact data, and use of this website's illustrative demo metrics as real outcomes. Each requires its own analysis and, where relevant, contract, notice and legal basis.
