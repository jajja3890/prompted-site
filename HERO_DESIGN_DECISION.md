# Prompted Product-first Hero — Design Review Round 2
**21 October 2026 | Preview-only concept | NOT APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION**

## What changed

The founder rejected the previous home hero because its right column was a largely empty dark block and the website did not immediately feel like a professional software company. Round 2 replaces that abstract method card with one intentional product-led composition.

- A clear, commercially understandable hero: **“Tekoäly osaksi oikeaa työtä.”** / **“Make AI part of real work.”**
- A legible desktop-browser illustration of Prompted Capability working with a person's real work task and digital coaching.
- A separate floating buyer Data illustration describing **published, access-controlled** group-level evidence, without any invented counts or fake customer results.
- A responsive mobile-browser concept showing **First signal → Repeat → Transfer → Independence**; not presented as a native mobile app.
- Light paper background, original Prompted wordmark, careful Acid Olive accent, and a balanced, high-legibility interface treatment rather than dead black space.
- Capability has its own corresponding heading and CTAs. Both FI/EN versions generated from the same maintained source.
- Existing product navigation, all other pages, legal/SEO prelaunch protections and product proof sections are preserved.

## Product truth and permissions

**Concept illustration, not screenshot.** The buyer reporting and participant evidence principles were cross-checked with the Prompted application's source before the previous round, but the layout here is original *marketing art direction*. It is NOT a pixel-for-pixel reproduction of the current authenticated UI. Do not present it as a live product screenshot, a client environment or verified customer outcomes. No third-party assets, copyrighted mockup kits, fictional customer logos or private transcripts are used.

## Browser/render evidence

- 16 FI/EN HTML pages rebuilt.
- 673 static, link, element-presence, and browser-layout checks passed, including 4 key pages at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 px.
- No observed browser JavaScript exceptions, missing target anchors or horizontal overflow at tested widths.
- 9 additional rendered screenshots produced, including three review targets (FI home desktop, FI home mobile, FI Capability desktop) and EN home screenshots.
- Screenshots come from in-memory Chromium rendering with local CSS/images embedded because local HTTP and file navigation are blocked in this execution environment. This **does not** replace live Vercel, physical hardware, Lighthouse, WCAG or screen-reader QA.

## Diff plan

New hero is generated by `product_first_hero(lang,page)` in `build.py`, and styled in `/assets/product-first.css`. No dependencies or cookies were added. Main `content.json`, other products, redirects, robots/noindex, Vercel and existing application source remain unchanged.

To rebuild: `python3 -m pip install -r requirements-build.txt && python3 build.py`.
To rerun browser checks in this environment: `python3 tools/qa_release_candidate.py`.
