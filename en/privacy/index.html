Privacy notice

This notice covers Prompted Oy's public prompted.fi marketing website and related enquiries. The authenticated app.prompted.fi customer service, learning activities and organisational reporting are covered by separate privacy information.

DRAFT / NOT YET LEGALLY APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION

Controller

Prompted Oy, Finnish business ID 3550114-7, Turku, Finland. Privacy questions and data subject requests: jarkko@prompted.fi. Prompted Oy is the controller for this marketing website and business enquiries addressed to it.

Personal data we process

If you contact us by email, we may process your name, email address, organisation, professional role, message and subsequent correspondence. Delivering the website may involve IP addresses, timestamps, requested URLs, browser and device information, and technical security and error logs. There is no contact form or personal user account on this site. The public Capability demonstration uses fictional information.

Purposes and legal bases

We process enquiries to answer questions, explore potential cooperation and prepare proposals you request. For representatives of organisations, this is generally based on our legitimate interests in appropriate B2B communications (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). If an individual requests steps relating to their own prospective contract, Article 6(1)(b) may apply where appropriate. We process necessary request data to deliver and secure the website and prevent misuse on the basis of legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Legitimate interests

Our interests are replying to enquiries initiated by you, managing appropriate business relationships and keeping the service secure. We limit processing to information necessary for these purposes. Our interests must be balanced against data subjects' rights and reasonable expectations. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Sources and voluntary provision

We receive enquiry information from you and from subsequent correspondence. Technical request information is generated when the service is used, including within the hosting infrastructure. Contacting us is optional. Without a functioning reply address we may be unable to answer your request.

Recipients and processors

Within Prompted Oy, only people who need enquiry information to perform their duties have access. The website is hosted by Vercel and corporate email is processed through the company's email provider. Providers and relevant subprocessors may process information to deliver their services under their agreements. Information may be disclosed to authorities when required by law. We do not sell personal data about website visitors or people contacting us.

Transfers outside the EEA

Vercel states that personal data related to its services may also be processed in the United States. Our email provider's supply chain may also involve international processing. Transfers outside the European Economic Area require an applicable adequacy decision or safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR, such as EU standard contractual clauses where necessary. Further information about the arrangements applicable to a transfer can be requested through our privacy contact.

Retention

We retain enquiry information for the time needed to handle the matter and explore potential cooperation. If no customer relationship results, our proposed retention practice is to delete or anonymise the enquiry within 12 months of the last substantive contact, unless longer retention is necessary, for example to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Separate legal obligations and retention criteria apply to customer records. Technical log retention follows the hosting settings in use and necessary security requirements.

Cookies and analytics

The application code for this marketing site includes no marketing analytics, advertising pixels or visitor profiling. The illustrative Capability experience runs in the browser without signing in and does not transmit demonstration choices to Prompted. Hosting infrastructure may process technical requests to deliver and protect the service. If we later introduce non-essential cookies or similar device technologies, we will describe them and obtain any necessary consent before use.

Information security

We restrict access to personal data to those who need it for their work and use protected connections as well as our providers' technical and organisational security measures. No email or online service is entirely risk-free. Please do not send sensitive personal information or confidential customer material by ordinary email without first agreeing on a secure channel.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and obtain data portability where its conditions are met. Any consent may be withdrawn at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact jarkko@prompted.fi. We generally respond within one month and explain any extension permitted by law. We may verify your identity.

Right to complain

You may complain to a competent supervisory authority, including in the EU Member State of your residence or workplace. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman, tietosuoja.fi. You may also contact us first to investigate your concern.

Automated decisions and separate services

This public website does not make solely automated decisions based on personal data that have legal or similarly significant effects, and does not profile visitors. The Capability demonstration is illustrative and does not process actual customer organisations' employee data. The authenticated coaching and customer service is subject to separate privacy information.

Changes to this notice

We update this notice if the website's features, providers or processing change materially. The current version will be published on this page. Prepared on 22 October 2026.
