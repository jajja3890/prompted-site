Website privacy

This draft notice concerns Prompted Oy’s public marketing website and the enquiries it generates. Separate privacy information applies to customer-facing and coaching services.

DRAFT / NOT YET LEGALLY APPROVED FOR PUBLICATION

Controller

Prompted Oy, business ID 3550114-7, Turku, Finland. Privacy enquiries: jarkko@prompted.fi.

Data categories

If you email us, we may process your name, email address, organisation and message. Website hosting may generate technical request logs, including IP addresses, timestamps and browser technical information. This website has no contact form.

Purposes and legal grounds — to verify

Enquiry information is used to answer questions, discuss proposals and prepare potential customer relationships. Depending on the circumstances, the proposed legal ground may be legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) or steps prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)). Confirm these grounds and the legitimate-interest assessment before publication. Technical data is processed for website availability and security.

Service providers and recipients — to verify

The website uses Vercel hosting. Enquiries are handled through the selected email service provider. Confirm each provider's role, any subprocessors and the relevant contractual arrangements before publication.

Retention — to verify

Messages are kept only as needed for the enquiry, a possible customer relationship or applicable legal duties. Technical log retention depends on the hosting provider. Confirm the specific retention periods or criteria before publication.

International transfers — to verify

Confirm whether hosting and email providers transfer information outside the European Economic Area and document relevant recipients and safeguards before publication.

Cookies and measurement — to verify

This website source version has no added marketing trackers, analytics scripts or profiling tools. Cookies and other device storage generated by the actual hosting environment must be checked before publication.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction, and object to processing. Data portability depends on the applicable legal ground. Contact jarkko@prompted.fi to exercise your rights. You may complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

Voluntary enquiries and automated decisions

Email enquiries are voluntary; without contact details we might not be able to respond. This marketing website does not make automated decisions about individuals or profile them. The Capability demo uses fictional sample data and collects no customer or coaching content.
