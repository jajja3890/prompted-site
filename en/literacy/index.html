WHY IT MATTERS

Everyone needs the basics. Not everyone needs to become an AI expert.

Before using a new tool, people should understand where it helps, what information they may enter and when its outputs need independent checking. The course offers an accessible foundation through short explanations, workplace scenarios and choices.

HOW THE COURSE WORKS

Short lessons. Your choices. A clear final assessment.

01 / LEARN

Work through four short modules: AI strengths and limits, risks, responsibility, and safe practical use.

02 / PRACTISE

Make decisions in fictional workplace situations and get clear, reasoned feedback. This is not an assessment of changed real-world work.

03 / CHECK UNDERSTANDING

Take a final knowledge assessment. A Prompted course-completion record is being prepared for successful learners, not an official certificate.

FOR WHOM

For the whole workforce. Beginners welcome.

Designed for specialists, customer support, sales, administration, managers and leaders – anyone using or preparing to use AI at work. No previous experience is needed. Employees learn at their own pace through short modules, scenarios and feedback. HR or business leaders can arrange the course for their teams.

BOUNDARIES

Foundational learning, not a compliance guarantee.

This training may support measures under Article 4 of the EU AI Act, but does not by itself establish full compliance or authorise a specific AI use case. The Finnish learner edition remains in editorial and technical pre-launch review.

Frequently asked questions

Do employees need any prior AI experience?

No. The course starts with the fundamentals and uses recognisable workplace situations.

Is there a certificate?

A Prompted course-completion record is being prepared for learners who pass the final assessment. It is not an EU or authority-issued certificate or a guarantee of organisational compliance.

Can our employees start immediately?

The first Finnish-language course edition is being prepared for pilots. Contact us about pilot availability. An English-language learner course is not yet available.

How does Literacy differ from Capability?

Literacy introduces the essentials of safe AI use in a short course. Capability supports longer-term changes to real work practices and evaluates those changes.
