PROMPTED / LITERACY · AI LITERACY

AI literacy for organisations that want to move forward responsibly.

Prompted Literacy helps companies establish a shared, work-relevant foundation for using AI and document their capability-building measures.

01 / THE CONTEXTREAL WORK

A generic course does not show how people use AI in their own work.

Different roles, tools, risks and everyday tasks call for different learning priorities. A credible programme starts from that context, not a one-size-fits-all certificate.

02 / THE APPROACHCONTEXT → PRACTICE → NEXT STEP
  1. 01 / CONTEXT

    Identify the AI tools, roles and learning needs relevant to the organisation.

  2. 02 / FOUNDATION

    Practise responsible use through real workplace examples, including human judgement and evaluation.

  3. 03 / DOCUMENTATION

    Document agreed measures and the areas that need further development.

WHO IT HELPS

Who is this for?

HR, People and L&D leaders, managers and business owners who need a practical starting point for strengthening workforce AI literacy.

HONEST SCOPE

AI literacy is not a compliance certificate.

The EU AI Act literacy obligation does not prescribe one universal official training programme or an authority-issued Prompted certificate. Organisations must assess suitable measures for their own context and risks. Scope is agreed before delivery.

Official regulatory reference: EU AI Act, Article 4 ↗

03 / COMMON QUESTIONSFAQ

Does Prompted Literacy guarantee legal compliance?

No. We support AI literacy measures and documentation, but do not provide a legal-compliance guarantee.

How does Literacy differ from Capability?

Literacy provides a common foundation for responsible AI use. Capability goes further by developing real work practices and evaluating the changes.
