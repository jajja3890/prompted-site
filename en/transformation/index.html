PROMPTED / TRANSFORMATION · ORGANISATIONAL CHANGE

AI transformation that changes how your organisation works.

Prompted Transformation connects leadership goals, practical capability development and evaluation of changing work. The engagement is designed around the organisation’s context.

Discuss organisational transformation ↗
01 / THE CONTEXTREAL WORK

New tools only create value when work changes.

Adoption alone is insufficient if good practices are not repeated, transferred between teams and sustained without constant external support. Change needs to be led from real work.

02 / THE APPROACHCONTEXT → PRACTICE → NEXT STEP
  1. 01 / PURPOSE

    Agree what needs to change in business outcomes and day-to-day work.

  2. 02 / CAPABILITY

    Connect learning and coaching to meaningful real-world tasks.

  3. 03 / EVIDENCE

    Examine repeatability, transfer and independence, then decide on the next steps.

WHO IT HELPS

Who is this for?

Business, transformation, People and L&D leaders seeking broader changes in ways of working than a stand-alone training programme can deliver.

HONEST SCOPE

Not a one-size-fits-all transformation package.

Transformation is a tailored collaboration model, not a standardised programme or guaranteed business result. Scope, ownership, measures and terms are agreed with each customer.

03 / COMMON QUESTIONSFAQ

How is this different from training?

The starting point is a business need. Development, support and evaluation take place in the context of actual workplace tasks.

Does Transformation have a fixed timeline or price?

Not at present. Scope and delivery are planned together and confirmed in a written proposal.
