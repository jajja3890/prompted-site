01 / PILOTBASELINE → PRACTICE → EVIDENCE

A pilot that is focused enough to start.

The tentative organisational evaluation window is around 90 days, with a typical cohort of around 20 participants. Precise scope, timing and price are agreed in the proposal. Ninety days is not a mandatory completion deadline for each learner.

  1. 01 / BASELINE

    Identify current practices and where change is needed.

  2. 02 / PRACTICE

    Participants build new ways of working in their own tasks, with digital coaching support.

  3. 03 / EVALUATION

    Build an evidence-based understanding of repetition, transfer and independence.

THE DELIVERY MODEL

Technology and people have different roles.

The digital coaching service supports participants in everyday tasks. A named Prompted contact supports the organisation and ensures the pilot remains tied to its priorities.
