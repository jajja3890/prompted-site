FOCUSED AND PRACTICAL

Not a one-off course. Not an open-ended transformation programme.

The initial pilot model is designed for around 20 participants. The organisational evaluation period is approximately 90 days, but individual learning is not tied to a mandatory completion deadline.

  • 01People, roles and work context
  • 02Support in real tasks
  • 03Bounded, reviewable evidence
  • 04A joint decision on next steps
SCOPE AND LIMITS

Pilot terms are agreed before delivery.

The tentative organisational evaluation window is around 90 days, with a typical cohort of around 20 participants. Precise scope, timing and price are agreed in the proposal. Ninety days is not a mandatory completion deadline for each learner. The pilot does not guarantee financial returns, individual credentials or immediate organisation-wide change.
