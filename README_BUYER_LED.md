# Buyer-Led Product Experience — corrected scope

The original five-step buyer-led experience was too broad and confused three products. The founder tested it and directed the change.

**Navigation:** separate commercial *Literacy / Capability / Transformation* and primary **Demo**.

**Demo = Capability only.** Two clear starting choices:
- **Haluan tutustua tilaajana / Explore as a buyer** (primary): indicative organisation service, what a buyer receives, privacy-scoped fictional Data/Evidence, optional calculator, pilot contact CTA.
- **Haluan kokeilla osallistujana / Try as a participant**: one workplace task, selected support need, different digital coaching next step and illustrative Evidence ladder.

No obligatory five-step wizard, no three-product role funnel. Real-client statistics and a live LLM are intentionally absent. Visual R3 product-first design stays intact. Both languages mirror the same flows.
