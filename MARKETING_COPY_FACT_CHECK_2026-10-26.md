# Prompted public marketing site — claims and commercial boundary audit

**26 October 2026 · Scope: new FI/EN static marketing website only · State: SOURCE-REVIEWED, NOT OWNER COMMERCIAL APPROVAL.**

## Reviewed content and claims

- **Three public offerings:** Literacy (responsible workplace AI literacy and documented measures), Capability (a defined, work-embedded organisational pilot), Transformation (tailored organisational change). They have distinct pages in both languages; only Capability has a **clearly labelled illustrative demo**. The demo does not claim to offer live authenticated coaching or actual customer reports.
- **EU AI Act / Literacy:** site explains that its activities may support a company's AI-literacy efforts. It explicitly **does not claim an official certificate or guarantee legal compliance**. No unsupported universal mandatory certificate claim found in the current `SERVICES` source entries.
- **Capability:** a **tentative organisational window around 90 days** and approximately **20 participants**; both languages explicitly say actual scope, price, dates and terms require a written proposal, and do not impose mandatory participant graduation at 90 days.
- **Data:** the source describes aggregate, bounded sponsor-level reporting rather than participant private conversations and raw text. This is an intended product/data-control statement: verify actual contractual access/reporting setup for the first real customer before advertising it as an independently audited security certification.
- **Buyer demo and impact calculator:** buyer-facing sample is marked **fictional**, and the optional capacity estimate is **assumption-driven, not measured ROI**. Participant experience is scripted, uses no customer work records or live AI and cannot generate learning evidence by clicking through.
- **Literacy/Transformation scope:** organisation-specific delivery and no fixed legal/commercial promise. No unverified actual ROI, funding, ranking, individual certificates or customer cohort results are implied by the reviewed core texts.
- **Commercial transaction path:** website has **no self-service order, checkout, card collection or on-page payment form**; current CTA is contact by email. Product/pilot scope, price, timings and obligations are agreed in a separate B2B offer. This is intentional marketing-site positioning and **not** evidence of a working payment method on the marketing site.
- **Corporate contact:** `jarkko@prompted.fi` was observed active in connected Gmail, and inbound/outbound business traffic exists. The review did not send a new deliverability test or certify every recipient environment.
- **Claim qualification:** sample figures, fictional data, indicative scope, coaching/support and lack of compliance guarantee must remain visible when generating the release bundle. Public references and individual customer logos/quotes are not authorised by this document.

## Decision boundary

This is a **factual/source integrity audit** of the candidate, not approval of exact brand voice, product maturity, availability to the next buyer, fulfillment contract or a client reference. The actual `commercial_copy` release gate remains **pending accountable owner acceptance of the FI/EN final commercial offer**. If service claims change, re-review the source and the actual Capability product delivery. Do not rewrite sensitive pricing or product claims without that context.
