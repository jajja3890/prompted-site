# Product and privacy boundary — design decisions

- This is Prompted's public sales **experience**, not a real logged-in Coach interface. It never sends user input to the product's Supabase tenant or calls the private Coach engine.
- Coach: uses hard-coded scenario/friction support decisions based on public product concepts. It is explicitly described as a deterministic walkthrough, not genuine AI inference.
- Evidence: clicking a stage explains what would constitute supporting evidence; clicking is **never** counted as learner evidence.
- Buyer Data: entirely invented group numbers 20 / 14 / 9 / 5, prominently labeled FICTIONAL DATA. These are not customer usage statistics, outcomes or sponsor reporting.
- Impact: hypothetical capacity = participants × minutes/week × working weeks ÷ 60; illustrated value = hours × assumed hourly rate. Costs, adoption, quality controls and causal measurement are intentionally excluded and prominently disclosed.
- Summaries stay client-side until the visitor explicitly downloads TXT; no analytics or account state is transmitted or persisted.
- Product offering: indicative Capability pilot is approximately 20 participants and 90-day *organisational* evaluation; never an individual learner deadline or a fixed contractual duration. No price claimed before an agreed offer.
- The page gives an accessible contact path only after users have explored value; no pre-trial email, calendar or sign-in gate.
- FI and EN parity is built and tested; no external widget, paid SaaS dependency, third-party iframe, live model cost or tracking pixel.
