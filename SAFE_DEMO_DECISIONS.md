# Capability public demo — data and commercial boundaries

1. **One demonstrable service:** Prompted Capability. Literacy and Transformation have their own pages but are not simulated using Capability functionality.
2. **Two audiences:** Buyer sees indicative delivery stages and a restricted illustrative Data/Evidence report first. Learner sees one example work task, and deterministic coaching that adapts to the selected support need. Switching is optional and reversible.
3. **No real customer data:** The buyer's example figures are completely fictional. They are NOT usage counts, customer metrics, financial returns or validated pilot results. Do not accidentally remove fictional labels.
4. **No private inference:** The demo's coaching moves are explicitly scripted decisions, not live calls to Prompted's actual authenticated Coach model or private intelligence engine. No API keys or tenant access in the browser.
5. **Evidence is not a click counter:** First signal, repeat, transfer, independence refer to real work evidence. Demo selections do not prove capability.
6. **Impact is optional and hypothetical:** Users can adjust number of participants, weekly minutes and working weeks; the calculation ignores costs, adoption, quality and causal attribution.
7. **No forced capture:** No account, email gate, third-party embed, advertising pixel, user session logging or persistent browser storage in demo code. Contact can follow value.
8. **No production promise:** Approx. 20 participants and indicative 90-day organisational pilot are hypothetical sales scope, not mandatory learner completion or a fixed contractual duration.
9. **Future real AI:** Requires its own approved server-side endpoint, quotas, budget controls, abuse protection, privacy review and safe fallback. Not in v2.
