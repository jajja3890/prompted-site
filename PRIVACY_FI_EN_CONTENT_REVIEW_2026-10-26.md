## Article 13 scope and corrected wording

Both languages have **14 matching privacy sections**. The site describes controller/contact, categories, Article 6 lawful bases, pursued legitimate interests, recipients, source and voluntary provision, extra-EEA transfers, retention criteria, cookies/analytics, data security, subject rights, complaints, automated decisions, and changes. Authenticated `app.prompted.fi` customer operations are expressly outside this notice.

Edits on the candidate revision:
- **Data-subject requests:** response without undue delay and no later than one month after receipt; permitted maximum two-month extension for complexity/volume with timely notice and reasons; identity confirmation only for reasonable doubts. No claim that applying a Gmail label resets the clock.
- **Transfers:** clearer reference to the actual Vercel-published SCC mechanism where applicable and how to request information on safeguards; Google Workspace's terms are described conditionally. No false EU-only, blanket US adequacy, or unconditional SCC claim.
- **Source version:** both privacy notices now identify the revised version as **26 October 2026**. Other website terms are not retroactively rewritten by a privacy-only review.
- **Retention:** owner approved a monthly **manual review**, not automatic deletion or a fixed email retention period; log retention uses actual criteria and no invented one-day universal deletion claim.
- **Security/cookies:** reviewed no-tracking static candidate with protected-preview SSO; this does not establish actual public-domain browser cookies or negate legitimate infrastructure logging.
