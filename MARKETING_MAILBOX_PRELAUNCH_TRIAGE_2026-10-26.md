## Method and findings

- Connected Gmail profile and live inbound/outbound activity were already verified on 26 October 2026, and the Google Workspace CDPA was accepted that day.
- Read-only searches inspected bounded, time-filtered **message metadata** for business-related terms in August–October 2026 and a narrow search for potential privacy-rights wording. Search terms can match hidden body text as well as subjects, so the result is **not a complete or clean classification** of marketing emails.
- The two time-window searches returned at least 25 and 23 records respectively, with further results indicated. Results included unrelated categories such as subscriptions, event invitations, account notifications and other correspondence, not just external business enquiries. **Do not infer 48 unique marketing enquiries**: the windows differ and the result categories are mixed.
- The rights-keyword query found two unrelated notifications rather than any obviously actionable data-subject request **within that very narrow query**. This does **not** establish that the entire inbox contains no rights requests.
- The Gmail `Prompted/Tietosuojapyynnöt` label was empty at the time of the sampled search; the label had only just been created for **future manual classification**. This is not proof of prior rights-request history.
- **No message was opened for full-body assessment in this review, or moved, labeled, marked read, deleted or archived.** No sender names, subjects, message IDs, text or personally identifying event details are copied into this public repository.

## Operational conclusion

The new `prompted-site` is still a protected, unlaunched website with **no form and no tracked inbox submission pipeline**. Historic mail arriving at the founder's business account cannot safely be assigned to the new site's processing purpose merely because it mentions Prompted. In particular, do **not** apply a new website-only email deletion rule to pre-existing personal, unrelated project, customer, legal, accounting or business-development records.

For the new site, **the first actual website-specific marketing enquiry and the first monthly case review can occur only when there are real relevant messages**. A realistic pre-launch requirement is a documented, accountable workflow ready for first use, not fabricated proof of having deleted an enquiry that does not yet exist.
