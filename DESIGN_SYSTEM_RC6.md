- One consistent product-page framework for **Literacy, Capability, Transformation**, with differentiated product signatures and the same content hierarchy.
- Unify logo alignment by preserving the **official** SVG without affine transforms, colour manipulations or replacement fonts. Remaining optical QA still requires design approval.

The image-generation portfolio study from the session is **not the website**: it contains invented company logos, synthetic interface previews and a serif direction contrary to the source brand system. **Never publish it or infer product evidence from it.**
- H01/H05 unapproved visual studies remain available offline; neither used in rendered pages. No generated visual assets are copied into the site.

## Art direction: two candidate image roles (not yet selected)
- H01 *Person and Place*: a singular editorial statement for the home (ONLY if it communicates human scale better than text and supports desktop/mobile cropping); no stock-office aesthetics, company logos or fake screenshots.

- Final choice requires source resolution, mobile/desktop crop checks, compression, image alt text and independent visual approval. Until then, intentional text-only pages are preferable.
