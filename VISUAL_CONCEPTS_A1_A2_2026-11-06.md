- Do **not** reproduce fictional analytics dashboard percentages, fake user results, supposed real client faces, invented performance claims, or generated lookalike logo lettering seen in the explorations.

## Concept A1 — Työ arjessa / HUMAN SCALE
**Meaning:** real work, learning and shared practices happen with actual people.
**Tone:** candid, warm daylight, tactile material, human proximity. Image compositions should give room to original headline and product UI.
**Visual formula:** editorial people-at-work visual + generous black/white/olive type + *existing* credible product explainer.
**Candidate first-wave locations:**
- Home: one carefully composed human-work visual as a distinct editorial moment below existing product-first hero.
- Literacy: strengthen accessibility and reduce friction through quiet typography, not another large photo.
- Company: original founder-approved identity section; only use real founder portrait if one is supplied/approved.
- Share card: type-led, optional small photo fragment with real brand master.
**Risks:** generic business stock or 'smiling people with laptops' can erase differentiation; AI-generated people must be clearly editorial, not implied testimonials.
**Acceptance test:** Does a People/L&D buyer immediately grasp practical employee change rather than generic corporate training?

## Concept A2 — Muutos ja suunta / HUMAN EVIDENCE
**Meaning:** leading durable business change with human capability as the mechanism.
**Tone:** bolder, spatial, structured, panoramic, editorial, controlled contrast; *avoid repeating the retired architectural cathedral hero*.
**Visual formula:** human-scale editorial geometry + one exceptionally clear operating-model diagram + actual product truth.
**Candidate first-wave locations:**
- Transformation: improved four-dimension strategic operating model (leadership → work → people → evidence) with strong visual rhythm.
- Home: no replacement of existing product first hero; at most a restrained directional graphic in supporting section.
- Share card: typographic editorial brand composition, not a dramatic generic building photograph.
**Risks:** abstract, cold 'consultancy transformation' impression; can drift away from real work or recreate discarded H01 architecture.
**Acceptance test:** Does an executive instantly understand the relationship between strategic direction, work redesign, capability and evidence?

## Comparative evaluation
| Criterion | A1 HUMAN SCALE | A2 HUMAN EVIDENCE |
|---|---|---|
| Human warmth | Strong | Moderate |
| Real-work clarity | Strong if activity is genuine | Moderate, requires concrete copy |
| Executive strategic feel | Moderate-high | High |
| Distinctiveness | Needs precise non-stock art direction | Strong only with original, purposeful system graphics |
| Product-first coexistence | Strong | Good if kept below hero |
| Main risk | Generic lifestyle photography | Return to generic monumental architecture |

## Working recommendation (not approval)
**A1 as the main editorial language** and **A2 as a limited Transformation/strategic information-design grammar**. Keep one unified Prompted brand, not two competing site themes. First wave should consist of:
